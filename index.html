FAQ

Computer repair questions, answered

How much does computer repair cost in Boca Raton?

At My IT Dudes, diagnostics are free in-shop. Most repairs cost between $79 and $250. Remote support starts at $49, virus removal at $99, laptop screens at $99 plus parts, and house calls at $119. You get a firm quote before any work begins.

Do you offer free diagnostics?

Yes. In-shop diagnostics are free, and we'll tell you what's wrong and what it costs before we touch anything. If you decide not to repair, you pay nothing.

How fast can you fix my computer?

Many repairs, like virus removal, tune-ups and SSD upgrades, are done the same day. Most hardware repairs take 1–3 days, depending on parts. Remote support is usually available the same day.

Do you come to my house?

Yes. We make house calls around Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Highland Beach, Boynton Beach, Deerfield Beach, Parkland, Coconut Creek and Coral Springs. Visits start at $119 and include the first hour.

Can you fix my computer remotely?

Yes. Remote PC repair works anywhere in South Florida and starts at $49. You approve the secure connection and watch everything we do. If we can't fix it remotely, you don't pay.

Do you repair Macs and gaming PCs?

Yes. We repair Windows PCs, MacBooks and iMacs, and we build and upgrade custom gaming PCs, including GPU installs, cooling, tuning and cable management.

Is it worth fixing my old laptop?

Usually, if the repair is under about half the cost of a comparable new computer. An SSD upgrade can make a 5–7 year old laptop feel new for around $150–$200 all-in. We'll tell you honestly when it's not worth it.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Labor is covered by a 90-day warranty, and parts carry the manufacturer's warranty.
