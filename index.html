FAQ

Questions, answered

What does My IT Dudes do?

My IT Dudes is a Boca Raton IT company. For businesses, we provide managed IT services, helpdesk support, cybersecurity, backup and disaster recovery, Microsoft 365, cloud services, VoIP phones and printer support. For homes, we handle PC and laptop repair, virus removal, data recovery, custom gaming PC builds, house calls and remote support.

What are managed IT services?

Managed IT services means we act as your outsourced IT department. We monitor your computers and network 24/7, keep everything patched and backed up, protect you from cyber threats and give your team a helpdesk to call, all under one ongoing agreement instead of paying for emergencies.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Most of our business clients are small and mid-sized offices in Boca Raton and South Florida, from 3-person practices to 100-user companies, including medical, dental, legal, financial, real estate and professional service firms.

How fast can you respond?

Most business support requests are handled remotely within minutes to a few hours, and on-site visits are usually available the same or next day around Boca Raton. Many home repairs, like virus removal and tune-ups, are done the same day.

Do you come on-site?

Yes. We visit offices and homes around Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Highland Beach, Boynton Beach, Deerfield Beach, Parkland, Coconut Creek and Coral Springs, and we support clients remotely across all of South Florida.

Can you fix my computer remotely?

Yes. Remote support works anywhere in South Florida. You approve the secure connection and watch everything we do. We never contact you through pop-ups or cold calls.

Do you repair Macs and build gaming PCs?

Yes. We repair Windows PCs, MacBooks and iMacs, and we build and upgrade custom gaming PCs, including GPU installs, cooling, tuning and cable management.

Can you help with HIPAA compliance?

Yes. We help medical, dental and other regulated offices with HIPAA risk assessments, encryption, access controls, secure email, backups and the documentation auditors ask for.
