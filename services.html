Service questions

What areas of South Florida do you serve?

We're based in Boca Raton. We provide on-site service from Boynton Beach to Coral Springs, and remote IT support everywhere in Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade counties.

Do I need a contract for business IT support?

We offer ongoing managed IT agreements for businesses that want proactive support, and on-demand help for those who just need a reliable IT partner to call. We'll recommend what fits your size and needs.

What brands and systems do you work on?

All major brands, including Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, MSI, Microsoft Surface, Apple and custom-built PCs, plus Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, QuickBooks, and common business printers and phone systems.

Will I lose my files during a repair?

Almost never. Most repairs keep your files intact. If a repair could affect your data, we'll back it up first and tell you before we start.

How do I know remote support is safe?

We only connect when you contact us or book us, never through a pop-up or cold call. You approve the connection, can watch the whole session and can end it at any time.
