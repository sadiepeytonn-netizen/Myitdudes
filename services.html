Service questions

What areas of South Florida do you serve?

We're based in Boca Raton. We do house calls from Boynton Beach to Coral Springs, and remote PC repair everywhere in Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade counties.

What brands do you work on?

All major brands, including Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, MSI, Microsoft Surface, Samsung, Alienware, Razer and Apple (MacBook and iMac), plus custom-built PCs.

Will I lose my files during a repair?

Almost never. Virus removal, tune-ups and most repairs keep your files intact. If a repair could affect your data, we'll back it up first and tell you before we start.

How do I know remote support is safe?

We only connect when you call us or book us, never through a pop-up or cold call. You approve the connection, watch the whole session and can end it at any time. Nothing stays installed afterward.

Do you buy parts, or can I bring my own?

Either works. We can source quality parts at fair prices, or you can bring your own for a custom build or upgrade. Labor pricing stays the same.
